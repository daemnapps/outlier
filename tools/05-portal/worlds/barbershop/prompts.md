# The Shop — the picture prompts

One prompt per beat, numbered to match the file the world expects in
`media/`. Generated in Supercomputer with Nano Banana Pro, 9:16, 2k, then
downsized to 810×1440 JPEG for the site (see `../../WHICH-MODELS.md`).

## The anchor sentence

Every prompt ends with this, word for word. It is what keeps nine
generations looking like one room. Never shorten it.

> Checkerboard floor, warm tungsten light, worn burgundy leather barber
> chairs, mirrors with photos taped to their edges. Shot on 35mm film,
> shallow depth of field, cinematic, photorealistic, no text overlays,
> no watermark.

## The barber, for consistency

Where he appears he is always: a Black man in his 40s with a
salt-and-pepper beard, black smock, gold chain. Generate his first frame
(02) and use it as the reference image for 04, 05, 06, 07.

Never the product. The player draws the product as a card from the
product file.

## 00-door.jpg

First-person POV: my own hand pushing open the glass front door of a
neighborhood Black barbershop on a Saturday morning, a small brass bell
above the door, a hand-lettered OPEN sign, warm tungsten light spilling
out over the sidewalk, inside I can see a row of guys waiting on a bench.
+ anchor

## 01-wait.jpg

First-person POV sitting on the waiting bench inside a neighborhood Black
barbershop: to my left a row of five Black men of different ages waiting,
phones out, one older man asleep with arms folded, a young boy swinging
his legs, ahead of us two barbers working at their stations, a wall
clock reading 10:40, a TV mounted high in the corner showing a basketball
game. + anchor

## 02-talk.jpg

Inside a neighborhood Black barbershop, mid-argument: the barber gestures
with his clippers at another barber across the room, both laughing, the
men waiting on the bench are leaning in and pointing, a TV in the corner
shows a basketball game. In the sharp foreground on the barber station: a
tall glass jar of bright blue Barbicide with black combs standing in it,
a talc brush, a spray bottle. + anchor

## 03-she-walks-in.jpg

Inside a neighborhood Black barbershop, seen from the waiting bench: the
front door has just swung open and a well-dressed Black woman in her
early 30s walks in holding the hand of her 7-year-old son who needs a
haircut, bright daylight behind her in the doorway, the men on the bench
have quietly straightened up and lowered their phones, one guy fixing his
collar, the barber pausing his clippers to greet them. Warm, respectful,
everyday, a little funny. + anchor

## 04-chair.jpg

First-person POV sitting down in a worn burgundy leather barber chair:
looking down past my own knees at the chrome foot pump and lever as the
barber's hand works the pedal to raise the chair, a black nylon cape
being snapped open in a blur beside me, the station in front with
clippers on hooks, a talc brush, a tall glass jar of bright blue
Barbicide with combs standing in it, mirror reflecting the shop behind.
+ anchor

## 05-mirror.jpg

Close-up in a barbershop mirror: the barber gently tilts the head of his
client, a Black man in his 30s draped in a black cape, two fingers under
the jaw, both looking at the client's neckline where the skin below the
beard line is bumpy and irritated with small razor bumps and a few dark
marks, the barber's expression is knowing and unbothered, the client's is
self-conscious. Tasteful. + anchor

## 06-station.jpg

Close-up of a barber's station: a hot white towel steaming, folded on the

straight razor closed, a shaving brush, a talc brush, a tall glass jar of
bright blue Barbicide with black combs standing in it, a spray bottle,
clippers hanging on hooks, the mirror behind reflecting the client in a
black cape. There is an empty clear space on the counter at front right.
No product labels. + anchor

## 07-aftershave.jpg

Close-up: the barber slaps aftershave from his cupped palms onto the
freshly lined-up neck and jaw of his client, a Black man in his 30s in a
black cape, who winces and grins at the sting, a crisp fresh lineup and
low fade visible, a small bottle of green aftershave open on the station.
+ anchor

## 08-exit.jpg

Late afternoon golden hour outside the barbershop: the client, a Black
man in his 30s with a crisp fresh low fade and lineup, walks out through

window and lightly touching his jawline, the shop's warm interior and
barber chairs visible through the glass behind him, long shadows, the
street quiet. + anchor
