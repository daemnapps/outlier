Find the place.

We are going to build a portal — see `the-format.md` — and the whole
thing stands or falls on one decision: **the physical place the customer
already goes to, where the problem is present.** Not a theme. Not a
metaphor. A room, a counter, a seat, a queue, with a smell and a wait and
people in it.

You are looking for a place with all five of these:

1. **They go there anyway.** It is already in their week or their month.
   Nobody has to be persuaded to be there.
2. **The problem shows up there on its own.** A mirror, a professional's
   hands, a moment of being looked at. The problem surfaces in the world
   without anyone bringing it up.
3. **Someone there has authority and no reason to lie.** The person who
   will hand over the lesson and, later, the product. A barber. A
   pharmacist. A coach. An older cousin.
4. **It has texture a regular would recognise and a stranger would not.**
   The details that prove the world is real. The objects nobody explains.
5. **It has a wait.** Time in which things can be said.

Read the avatar's *experiences*, *behaviors* and *cultural-context*
sections first. The place is in there, or it is not and you say so.

Three candidates, best first. For each:

```
**Evidence.** ⟨Every line above that came from the profile or the language

so the brand can confirm or cut it.⟩
```

Then one line: which of the three you would build, and the single reason.

# THE RULES

**From the folder, not from you.** A place the profile does not point to
is a guess. Guesses are marked. If the profile has nothing — no
experiences, no behaviors — stop and say: *the profile does not say where
this person goes. Fill in experiences and behaviors and run this again.*

**No brand places.** The brand's own store, website or event is never a
candidate. The customer goes to the place for their own reasons.

**No fantasy.** Nothing the customer has not actually stood in. The
portal works because they know the room better than we do.

**The problem must surface on its own.** If the only way the problem
comes up is someone asking about it, the place is wrong.
