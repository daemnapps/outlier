Write the world bible.

This is the document every beat, every line and every picture will be cut
from. It is written the way a regular would write it if you asked them to
describe the place to someone who has never been — not a set designer, not
a brand, a regular. Past tense, first person plural where it fits ("you
already know"), in the customer's own register from the language file.

# THE SHAPE

```
# ⟨The place, as they call it⟩

## Arriving
⟨The approach, the door, the first three seconds. What you hear before
you see. What everybody does when the door opens.⟩

## The room
⟨Walk it. Floor, chairs, mirrors, walls, what's taped to what. The light.
The TV and what is on it. Where people sit and where they never sit.⟩

## The objects nobody explains
⟨Eight to twelve. Each one a name and one sentence about what it means to
someone who has been here a hundred times. These become the hotspots.⟩

## The people
⟨The person with authority: what they are called, how they talk, what
they call you, what they never say. Two or three regulars, by role, not
name. Anyone whose arrival changes the room.⟩

## The wait
⟨How long. What fills it. The argument that is always going on. What the
clock does.⟩

## The talk
⟨Twelve to twenty lines that get said in this room. Real register. Some
funny. None of them about the product. At least four from the language
file, quoted.⟩

## The turn
⟨The one moment in the visit when the problem surfaces on its own. Who
notices. What the customer does with their hand, their collar, their eyes.
What is said, or not said. Write this one slowly.⟩

## The hands
⟨What the person with authority does to you. Touch, tools, order of
operations. The sounds. Where it stings.⟩

## Leaving
⟨The last thing said. The door. The reflection. What you check in the
first window you pass.⟩

## Evidence
⟨Every detail above that came from the profile, the language file or the
brand's own notes: cite it. Every detail that came from general knowledge
of such places: mark it "⚠ confirm". The brand reads this list and cuts
what is wrong.⟩
```

# THE RULES

**Nouns over adjectives.** "The blue jar the combs live in" beats "an
authentic, lived-in atmosphere". If a sentence has no object in it, cut it.

**No product.** The product does not exist in this document. Its place is
reserved in *The hands* — an unnamed "something he keeps on the station"
— and that is all.

**No pitch.** Nobody in this world sells anything. The person with
authority is good at their job and talks like it.

**Their words.** Every spoken line passes the language file. Address
terms, connectors, comparisons — from there, never from you. No medical
terms. No marketing terms. No em dashes.

**The turn is the reason the document exists.** If it is not
specific enough to make the reader touch their own neck, it is not done.

**Length.** Eight hundred to fourteen hundred words. Long enough to cut
from, short enough that the brand reads it all.
