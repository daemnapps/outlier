# premiere-line

The direct line into Adobe Premiere Pro: read the live timeline, place and trim
clips, apply effects and keyframes, add transitions, insert graphics, queue
exports — as normal undoable edits on an open project.

Moved from Damon's own repo 2026-09-20. `README.md` beside this is the original.

**Where it sits next to the video edit.** `video-edit` is the
programmed edit — a cut sheet renders itself and nobody opens an editor. This
is the opposite door: for the times a human is in Premiere and wants the
session to do the work in front of them. Keep both; they answer different asks.

Needs: Premiere running, and the bridge extension installed (`install.sh`,
`daemn-bridge.ccx`).
