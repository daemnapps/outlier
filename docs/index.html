What the machine actually does

Three moves, over and over.

Nothing here is clever. It is the same three moves run at a volume a person cannot match by hand.

01 · Read it

Tear the thing down

A post that already worked goes in. What comes out is its structure — the hook, the beats, the argument — separated from the brand that made it.

02 · Turn it

Put our brand in it

The same surface, turned. The structure holds; the problem, the product and the words become ours. Nothing is invented that the market did not say.

03 · Split it

One brief, many assets

A brief fans out — hooks, variations, formats, a whole batch — each one a real test rather than four unrelated pictures.

The output

It ends in a brief someone can shoot.

Not a strategy deck. A document a clipper or a creator opens, matches against the post it came from, and makes.

Every brief shows what it was built from, carries its own Google Doc and a Drive folder with the deliverables, and names the offer, the product and where the traffic goes.

Why it keeps working

The loop is the product.

When a brief lands, the machine makes more like it. When one is wrong, one prompt gets fixed and every brief after it is better.

That is the whole reason the prompts are readable files rather than a black box — the thing you improve is the instruction, not the output.
