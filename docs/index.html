01  What it is

A marketing machine you run yourself

It takes a brand, learns how its customers actually talk, and produces the work — static ads, video scripts, advertorials, landing pages, quiz funnels, a month of email, post-purchase paths.

It also does competitor intelligence: point it at a rival's domain and it pulls their entire live ad library, works out which handful of angles they are genuinely spending behind, and hands you the teardown.

Everything runs locally in Claude Code. Nothing is hosted by me. There is no account, no dashboard, no server holding your data — you clone a repository and it runs on your machine with your own API keys.

This is not a product. It's the actual working directory I use, cleaned up enough to hand over.

03  What to expect

Honestly

  1. A weekend to get comfortable. Not an afternoon. The first run is rough and the second is noticeably better, because it keeps your corrections.
  2. You still edit everything. It gets you from blank page to a real draft with evidence behind it. It does not get you to publish.
  3. It costs money to run. Not to me — to the model providers. See below.
  4. Some of it will break. These are live experiments, not a maintained product. That's the trade for getting them early.

What it will not do

Publish anything. Spend anything on ads. Invent a customer quote. Make a medical claim. Write in a voice it hasn't been given evidence for. Every one of those is a hard stop in the prompts, not a guideline.

Who this is for

Operators and marketers who already know what good work looks like and want to stop producing it one piece at a time. If you're looking for something that thinks for you, this will disappoint you.

And here is exactly how I make money on it

The code is free and always will be. I'm not running a business off my friends.

But the machine burns credits at the model providers — Claude for the writing, Higgsfield for image and video generation. I use affiliate links for those tools. If you sign up through them, I get a cut of what you spend, at no extra cost to you.

That's the whole business model. I'd rather tell you that on the pricing section than bury it in a footer.

Why that's still cheaper for you

A month of AI generation costs less than a single UGC creator shoot, and produces more usable variants. The credits are the real cost of running this — the software around them is free.

If you'd rather not use my links, don't. Sign up directly and the tools work identically. Nothing is gated.

No email list, no phone number, no group chat. I'm not building a database and I'm not answering the same thing forty times in a chat window. Answered in the open, once, where the next person finds it.
