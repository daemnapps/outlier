The four questions, every time
What does the camera do?Handheld, subject height, never cuts to a face.
What does the text do?One line, pinned, never changes.
What order are the beats in?Sealed → unfolded → placed → used → the mechanism.
What is doing the persuading?The unbroken run of hands. Nothing is argued; it is all shown.

Why it works — no cut to a talking head means the viewer is never asked to grant a stranger their attention. They just watch a pair of hands open something.

04  What to expect

Read this
before you
download

Honest about what this is: a system that does the thinking between a video that worked and a brief someone can shoot. It is worth your time if you are already making creative and losing to the blank page.

  • What it isA process you follow, not a button you press. You bring a video and your brand; it does the watching, the structuring and the writing — and tells you when it does not know something instead of making it up.
  • What you needClaude (the chat or Cowork), Higgsfield Supercomputer, and a Google Drive. That is the whole list — nothing gets installed and nothing runs on your computer.
  • What it will not doInvent your product, your customer or your offer. Every gap in your brand folder shows up as a question, never as a confident guess.
  • The rule that makes it workRebuild the structure, never the content. A scalp treatment and a countertop demo can be the same format — that is the whole point.
  • How long it takesYour brand folder is an afternoon, once. After that a teardown is about twenty minutes of pasting, plus however long you spend generating scenes.
  • What it costsNothing. MIT licensed, and it stays that way.

05  The production step

Two of the seven steps happen in Higgsfield Supercomputer: it is where the video actually gets watched (it has a Gemini model on it, so you hand it the video directly) and where your scenes get made from the prompts the brief writes.

Nothing here is locked to it — the prompts are plain description and work in any generator. Supercomputer is what these instructions are written for, so it is the path with no guesswork.
