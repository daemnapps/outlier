So the format is
Caught-in-the-act misread
one of 34 formats in the pack · each with the post that proved it

Someone walks in on a scene that looks like something it is not. The argument runs while the real explanation — the product — waits until the last beat.

Why it works — the viewer knows something the character does not, and cannot leave until the misunderstanding lands. The product arrives as the answer, not as an interruption.

04  How to use it

How you
actually
use this

If you can save a file to Google Drive and paste text into a chat box, you can run this. No experience needed.

Pick a brand you make ads for, fill in its folder once, then go as hard as you want. Every video you turn around is one they did not have to brief, storyboard or shoot.

  • The loopFind a video that did well. Feed it in. Get back a script and a shot list. Make the shots, cut it, send it. Then do it again — there is no limit and no approval step in the middle.
  • What you needThree things: Claude (the free chat is fine), Higgsfield Supercomputer, and a Google Drive. Nothing gets installed and nothing runs on your computer.
  • Where people go wrongSkipping the brand folder. If it does not know what the product looks like, it will say so instead of guessing — that is it working, not breaking. Go fill that part in and it stops happening.
  • The one ruleCopy the shape, not the content. A hair product and a frying-pan oil can use the exact same video structure. That is the whole trick.
  • How fastThe brand folder is one afternoon, once per brand. After that, about twenty minutes per ad plus generating time. That is the whole reason this exists — volume without a meeting.
  • What it costs youNothing. All four downloads are free. The only thing you might pay for is Supercomputer once you are generating at volume.

05  The one tool you need

What is
Supercomputer?

What it is. A chat where you make things. You type what you want — or drop a file in and ask about it — and it does the work: images, video, edits. If you have used ChatGPT it will look familiar; the difference is this one makes pictures and video, not just words.

Why you need it here. Two of the seven steps happen in it, and they are the two a normal chat cannot do.

Which model? You do not pick one. There is a setting called Auto that chooses for you, and for both of these steps Auto is the right answer. Ignore every other setting until you have made something.

What it costs. There is a free tier to start on. Everything on this page is free either way — Supercomputer is the only thing you might ever pay for, and only once you are generating at volume.
