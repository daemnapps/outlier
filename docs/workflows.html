The four questions, every time
What does the camera do?Handheld, subject height, never cuts to a face.
What does the text do?One line, pinned, never changes.
What order are the beats in?Sealed → unfolded → placed → used → the mechanism.
What is doing the persuading?The unbroken run of hands. Nothing is argued; it is all shown.

04  Is this for you

Is this
for you?

If you can save a file to Google Drive and paste text into a chat box, you can run this. No experience needed, and nothing to install.

What you are learning is the actual job: take something that already worked, work out why, and rebuild it for someone else's product. Brands pay for that.

  • What you actually doFind a video that did well. Feed it in. Answer nothing — the brand folder already did that. Get back a script and a shot list, then make the shots. That is the loop.
  • What you needThree things: Claude (the free chat is fine), Higgsfield Supercomputer, and a Google Drive. Nothing gets installed and nothing runs on your computer.
  • Where people go wrongSkipping the brand folder. If it does not know what your product looks like, it will say so instead of guessing — that is it working correctly, not breaking. Go fill that part in.
  • The one ruleCopy the shape, not the content. A hair product and a frying-pan oil can use the exact same video structure. That is the whole trick.
  • How long it takesThe brand folder is one afternoon, once. After that, about twenty minutes per video plus whatever you spend generating scenes.
  • What it costsAll four downloads are free forever. The only thing you might pay for is Supercomputer, and only once you are generating a lot.

05  The one tool you need

What is
Supercomputer?

What it is. Higgsfield Supercomputer is a chat where you make things. You type what you want, or upload a file and ask about it, and it does the work — images, video, edits. If you have used ChatGPT, it looks familiar; the difference is that it makes pictures and video rather than just text.

Why you need it here. Two of the seven steps happen in it. First, it is where the video gets watched — you upload the clip and ask it to describe every scene, and a normal chat window cannot do that. Second, it is where your scenes get made from the prompts the brief writes.

Which model? You do not pick one. It has a setting called Auto that chooses for you, and for these steps Auto is the right answer. Ignore every other setting until you have made something.

What it costs. There is a free tier to try it and paid plans if you make a lot. Everything on this page is free either way — Supercomputer is the only thing you might pay for, and only when you are generating.

Nothing here is locked to it. The prompts are plain description and work in any generator. This is just the path with no guesswork.
