01  How it works

Swipe → Scene

Five steps. The first four are done for you and sitting in the download; the fifth is where your product goes in.

STEP 01

Swipe

A post that already worked gets saved. Not an ad someone paid to show you — organic content real people chose to watch.

STEP 02

Strip

Six frames pulled across its runtime. Enough to read the structure without watching a thing.

STEP 03

Name the structure

What the camera does, what the text does, the beats in order, and what carries the persuasion. Same four questions every time — which is why the same structure keeps showing up under different topics.

STEP 04

Write the prompts

An image prompt and a motion prompt for the beat that matters, with your product and person left as blanks. Nothing invented — a placeholder is a question, not a guess.

STEP 05

Generate and cut

Fill the blanks, generate the scenes, cut to the beat list. Same shape that already earned the views, wearing your product.

03  What to expect

Read this
before you
download

This is a swipe file with the reasoning attached, not a button that makes ads. It is worth your time if you are already making creative and losing to the blank page.

  • It gives youThe structure that earned the views, in enough detail to rebuild it — and the prompts to generate it rather than shoot it.
  • It does not give youYour product, your customer, or your offer. Those are yours, and every prompt leaves a blank where they go.
  • The rule that makes it workRebuild the structure, never the content. A scalp treatment and a countertop demo can be the same format — that is the whole point.
  • The rule that keeps it honestNothing invented. If a brief does not say how the product looks or what the person is wearing, ask. A generated frame that guesses is worse than no frame.
  • What it costsNothing. It is MIT licensed and it stays that way.

04  The production step

Where the scenes
get made

The kit ends with a prompt. Something has to turn that prompt into a shot — I use Higgsfield for that, and the prompts in every sheet are written in its shape: an image prompt for the frame, a motion prompt for what the camera does.

Nothing here is locked to it. The prompts are plain description and work in any generator you already pay for.
