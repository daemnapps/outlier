Two lanes, one formula

Slideshows

TikTok photo-mode, 5–9 slides. Slide 1 is a collage or a close-up of the problem; the product slide is required; the last slide carries the CTA. Tye's formats A / B / C are on the Tye's Clipping Briefs page.

Video

Short vertical video, 9:16. Routines, transformations, product proof, barbershop. Video travels further than a slideshow — same formula, in motion. The video swipes and video briefs here are the starting set; the machine keeps adding.

How to get in — clippers and creators

  1. Join the Whop. Every clipper and every creator posts and gets paid through it: whop.com/motionclipping. Post → copy the link → submit it in the Content Rewards tab → paid to your Whop balance 4 days after approval. TikTok Shop commission stacks on top.

2. THE FRAME MECHANICS

The reading path

  1. Background Image (establishes context/subject).
  2. Zone A (delivers the step/hook).

Viewer understands the visual context instantly, then reads the specific directive.

The scroll-stop mechanism Aspirational face/jawline (Slide 1). Mechanism: Face and eye contact (implied) + high-contrast native text promising a specific, highly desired physical transformation. Second strongest: The "before/after" implication of Slide 2.

Functional zones

  • Block name: The Hook (Slide 1)
  • Mechanical purpose: Arrest scroll, state exact value proposition.
  • Why it works: Direct "Things I did" implies proven, personal success. Breaks if cut: Viewer has no context for the subsequent list.
  • What it sets up: The Proof.
  • Block name: The Proof (Slide 2)
  • Mechanical purpose: Validate the hook's promise visually.
  • Why it works: Shows the result (sharp jawline) without text, forcing focus on physical evidence. Breaks if cut: Lack of credibility.
  • What it sets up: The Protocol.
  • Block name: The Protocol (Slides 3-9)
  • Mechanical purpose: Deliver promised steps.
  • Why it works: Rapid-fire, simple, actionable directives paired with literal imagery. Breaks if cut: Fails to deliver promised value.
  • What it sets up: Save/Share action.

Copy beats Line: "Things I did to get a sharper jaw line" Device: The Personal Blueprint. Why it lands: Targets insecure young men seeking physical improvement; implies a proven, accessible system rather than genetics. Trigger words: "Things I did", "sharper jaw line".

Hierarchy and weight 80% image, 20% text. Single type size. High contrast (white text/black stroke) ensures legibility over any background. Text survives at 25% scale.

3. THE PSYCHOLOGY

  • The thesis: "The Attainable Glow-Up Protocol."
  • Character archetype: "The Aspirational Peer." Built by casual wardrobe (hoodie), everyday settings, native selfie angles. He is a regular guy who "figured it out."
  • Aesthetic archetype: Native TikTok/Reels educational carousel. Borrows credibility from the platform's organic, user-generated content format.
  • The setting: Mundane, everyday locations (garage, gym, kitchen) reinforce that this protocol requires no special facilities or wealth.
  • The psychological plays:
  • The Curiosity Gap. "Things I did..." forces a swipe to see the list. Belief shift: Viewer believes a hidden method exists.
  • The Scientific Veneer. Mixing basic habits (water) with niche tools (Gua sha) and anatomical terms (potassium). Belief shift: Viewer believes this is a biologically sound protocol, not just a cosmetic trick.
  • The Effort Illusion. 7 distinct steps make the result feel earned and systematic. Belief shift: Viewer believes the result is guaranteed if they follow the checklist.
  • The viewer's end state: Motivated, equipped with a checklist, likely to save the post for future reference.

4. THE INDEX

Definitions index

  • The Personal Blueprint: Framing advice as a personal historical record rather than a generic tutorial (Working label).
  • The Curiosity Gap: Withholding actual information to force an interaction/click (Established term).
  • The Scientific Veneer: Using clinical/anatomical language to elevate basic advice (Working label).

Element registry

  • Native Text Block: Centered text, white with black stroke, ~4% frame height, Zone A.
